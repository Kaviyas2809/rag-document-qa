# Acme Logistics — Monthly Operations Report (October 2026)

## Summary
Acme Logistics completed another strong month in October 2026. Total shipments
dispatched reached 14,586 units across four zones: South, East, North and West.
The company operates 30 sales offices nationwide.

## Collection
Total collection for the month was 144.53 crore against a plan of 144.53 crore,
achieving 100% of target. The South zone led with 39.08 crore, followed by the
East zone at 40.19 crore. The highest-performing single office was WB with 12.27
crore in collections.

## Billing
Monthly billing reached 148,749 units. The billing achievement stood at 43% of
the full-month plan as of the reporting date. The South zone contributed the
largest share of billed volume.

## Despatch
Despatch plan for the month was 480,000 units. Actual month-to-date despatch was
62,053 units. The daily despatch plan was 17,000 units, and on the reporting day
14,586 units were actually despatched, an achievement of 86%.

## Fleet and Delivery
The fleet operated reliably with a 95% on-time delivery rate. Average time to
resolve a pipeline failure improved from 8 hours to 5 hours during the quarter.
No major outages were recorded in October.

## Outlook
November 2026 targets a 5% increase in despatch volume and continued investment
in electric vehicle (EV) logistics, which grew 12% quarter over quarter.
